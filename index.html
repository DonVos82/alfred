Personal email assistance

A personal assistant for your inbox.

Alfred helps Don find and understand Gmail messages and prepare email drafts for review. It runs on a privately operated server and uses an AI provider to help answer requests.

How Alfred helps

Ask Alfred to search a connected mailbox, summarise relevant messages, identify items needing attention, or prepare and revise a draft in Gmail.

Alfred is accessed through a private interface or an authorised Telegram conversation. This information website has no mailbox login, upload form or public assistant service.

How a mailbox is connected

  1. Don chooses a Gmail account he is authorised to use.
  2. Google presents the permissions requested by Alfred. Don grants access through Google.
  3. Alfred uses that permission to read relevant messages and manage drafts when requested.

The same information pages can describe additional authorised accounts. Each account requires its own consent and a separately configured connection; publishing these pages does not connect another mailbox.

You make the final decision

The installed Gmail tool has no send or email-delete command. Don reviews recipients, wording and facts in Gmail and sends messages himself. Google’s draft permission also includes sending capability; the current tool implements the narrower draft workflow.

AI summaries and drafts can contain mistakes. Check important details against the original message.

Who operates Alfred?

Alfred is a personal setup operated by Don. It is not offered as a public email service and is not affiliated with Google or OpenAI.
