Data handling

Privacy policy

This policy explains how Alfred handles Gmail data for the accounts Don explicitly connects.

Last updated 29 October 2026 · Operator: Don · Contact: brendonwork@gmail.com

1. Data Alfred accesses

After Google authorisation, Alfred can access the connected account’s email address, message identifiers, senders, recipients, subjects, dates, labels, snippets and message text. It can read and manage email drafts. The current Gmail tool does not provide a separate attachment-download command.

Reading is directed by Don’s requests. The account permission itself is broader than an individual search: it permits reading mail across the authorised account.

Google permissionPurpose and boundary
gmail.readonlyFind and read messages and associated mailbox information.
gmail.composeRead, create and revise drafts. Google also includes sending in this permission; the installed Alfred Gmail tool has no send command.

These permissions do not grant access to Calendar, Drive or Contacts. Additional services or permissions require a separate setup and updated disclosure.

2. Why the data is used

Alfred uses relevant Gmail information to respond to Don’s requests, summarise messages, identify matters needing attention and prepare drafts. It keeps limited operational records to track drafts, preserve prior draft versions and support the assistant’s operation.

Alfred is not operated to sell email data, display advertising, build advertising profiles or provide data to brokers.

3. AI processing and connected services

Alfred currently uses OpenAI through the Codex connection. Relevant email text, headers, conversation context and draft content can be sent to OpenAI to produce answers and drafts. This processing happens outside the privately operated server.

When Don uses Telegram, his requests and Alfred’s replies pass through Telegram; replies may include requested email summaries or draft text. The private browser interface is an alternative.

Google processes Gmail API requests and stores mailbox messages and drafts. Each provider’s own terms, privacy practices and account controls apply to its service.

AI data controls

On 29 October 2026, Don disabled “Improve the model for everyone” in the ChatGPT account he confirmed Alfred uses. The setting was checked again after reloading. This account setting alone does not establish the exact training or retention behaviour of every part of Alfred’s connection.

OpenAI processes submitted email context under the terms and account controls applicable to that connection. This policy does not promise zero provider retention or a fixed provider-deletion period.

Provider information: OpenAI privacy policy, Telegram privacy policy and Google privacy policy.

4. Storage, security and retention

Google authorisation credentials are kept in restricted files on the privately operated server. They are not included in these website files or the public website repository.

Gmail remains the mailbox store. Alfred’s server also retains draft tracking information, previous draft versions, assistant conversation and tool history, and operational records. Email content processed in a conversation may therefore remain in that history; summaries or preferences may be retained in assistant memory.

Automatic deletion limits have not been verified for every record type, so this policy does not promise a fixed retention period for local records or backups. Removing Google access stops future authorised requests but does not automatically erase existing server records, Gmail drafts or copies processed by service providers.

5. Control, revocation and deletion requests

Don can stop using Alfred and remove its Google access through Google Account connections. This does not delete the Gmail account or its messages.

For removal of local Alfred records, contact the operator. Local records and backups must be reviewed separately from Google and other provider-held data. No automatic erasure deadline is promised by this policy.

Each additional mailbox requires explicit Google consent and a correctly configured connection. Its data is covered by these disclosures when that connection is enabled.

6. These public information pages

The supplied website files contain no mailbox contents, OAuth credentials, message identifiers or server configuration. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, forms or cookies set by the pages.

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes website requests and may record visitor information such as IP addresses for security under its own privacy statement. Visiting these pages does not grant the website host Gmail access.

7. Google data-use commitments and changes

Google-derived data is intended only for the personal assistance features described here. Alfred’s use and transfer of Google API data must follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Material changes to permissions, processing providers or data uses require an updated policy and any consent Google requires. Contact: brendonwork@gmail.com.
