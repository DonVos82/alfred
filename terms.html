Personal use

Terms of use

Alfred is a private assistant operated by Don. These terms describe its intended use and the review expected before acting on its output.

Last updated 29 October 2026 · Read together with the privacy policy.

Who may use Alfred

The live assistant is intended for Don and accounts he is authorised to connect. These public information pages are not an invitation to register, connect a mailbox or use a shared public assistant.

Each connected Google account must be controlled by its owner or connected with appropriate authority and separate Google consent. Additional connections must be configured and checked before use.

What Alfred provides

The current Gmail workflow supports searching and reading messages and creating or revising tracked, unsent drafts. The installed Gmail tool does not provide email-send or email-delete commands.

The permissions Google grants may cover capabilities beyond the current tool’s commands. In particular, Google’s compose permission includes sending as well as draft management. The privacy policy explains this boundary and how email context is processed.

Review before acting

AI-generated summaries, suggested actions and drafts can be incomplete or incorrect. Don reviews original emails, attachments where relevant, recipients, dates, amounts and wording before relying on a summary or sending a draft.

Draft preparation is not approval to send a message, make a payment, accept an obligation or change an account. Decisions and any final email submission remain with Don.

Availability and security

Alfred depends on the private server, network, Google authorisation and other service providers. Access can expire or be revoked, and the service may be interrupted. It is not an emergency service or a guaranteed reminder system.

Keep credentials private and use the authorised interfaces. Report suspected unauthorised access to the operator. Review relevant provider terms for Google, OpenAI, Telegram and the website host.

Ending access and making changes

Don may stop the assistant or revoke Google access at any time. Existing drafts and stored records are handled separately, as explained in the privacy policy.

The operator will update these information pages before materially expanding the disclosed data uses or opening access to other users. These terms do not assert Google approval, verification or certification.
